About the Portal

This demonstration portal helps citizens submit public grievances and follow their progress through a structured review process.

How It Works

1. Register

Create a citizen account to access the portal.

2. Submit

Submit your grievance with relevant details.

3. Track

Track the status and department updates.

4. Resolution

View the action taken and provide feedback.

Departments

Grievances can be directed to the relevant departments for review and action.
